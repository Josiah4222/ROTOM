Challenges that rotom ethiopia faces

Understanding the operational hurdles we work tirelessly to overcome

Funding Limitations

We rely on donations, and financial constraints often limit our ability to enroll new seniors from our growing waiting list.

Rising Inflation

The soaring cost of food, fuel, and basic supplies in Ethiopia puts a heavy strain on our operational budget.

Facility Capacity

Our current center is at maximum capacity, preventing us from providing 24/7 care to more homeless seniors who need it.

Medical Supply Scarcity

Frequent shortages and rising prices of essential medications make it difficult to guarantee consistent treatment.

Increasing Demand

Every day we identify more elders living in destitute conditions, but our resources restrict the number we can support.

Infrastructure Gaps

Limited vehicle resources and poor road conditions make it challenging to reach home-based seniors in remote areas.
