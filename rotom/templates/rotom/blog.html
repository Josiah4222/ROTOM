Key Challenges Facing Elders

Understanding the critical issues that affect elderly people in Ethiopia

Poverty & Inflation

Struggling to meet basic needs such as food, shelter, and clothing due to rising costs and limited income sources.

Limited Healthcare Access

Limited access to essential medical services, increasing vulnerability to illness and disease without proper treatment.

No Pension System

Financial insecurity in old age for those engaging in informal work with no retirement benefits or social security.

Caregiving Burden

Serving as primary caregivers for grandchildren without adequate income, sustaining the cycle of poverty across generations.

Social Isolation

Facing social isolation and feelings of loneliness, leading to mental health challenges and decreased quality of life.

Homelessness Risk

Homelessness often caused by rural-to-urban migration for low-wage jobs, leading to living on the streets in old age.

How ROTOM Addresses These Challenges

Our comprehensive approach to supporting Ethiopia's elderly community

Center-Based Care

Comprehensive daily care, meals, medical support, and social activities for 30+ seniors in our dedicated facility.

Home-Based Support

Regular home visits, food assistance, medical supplies, and home improvements for 120+ elders in the community.

Educational Programs

School support, supplies, uniforms, and mentorship programs affecting 200+ grandchildren and young people.

Healthcare Services

Medical checkups, medications, therapy services, and emergency healthcare interventions for vulnerable seniors.

Volunteer Training

Training programs, materials, and coordination for 500+ volunteers who extend our reach into the community.

Infrastructure Development

Facility expansion, equipment upgrades, and technology improvements to serve more elders effectively.
