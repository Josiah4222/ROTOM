Our Initiatives

ROTOM Ethiopia is dedicated to empowering grandchildren and girls through comprehensive educational support, fostering dignity, empowerment, and community:

School and Personal Need Support

Providing school tuition, supplies, and personal necessities to ensure grandchildren and girls have access to quality education and proper care.

Home and School Visits

Conducting regular follow-ups to monitor student progress and provide necessary assistance for academic success.

Personal Mentorship

Assigning mentors to challenge and inspire students, supporting their personal growth and development.

Training and Nurturing Initiatives

Offering training programs to enhance students’ skills and prepare them for a successful future.

Breaking the Cycle

Getnet Belay

University Student, Grandson of Mamo Bekele

"Getnet Belay, the grandson of Mamo Bekele and a remarkable symbol of hope, has transformed his life through education, becoming the first in his family to attend university and breaking generational barriers. We first met him in 2017 when he was just 10 years old and in fourth grade. Since then, ROTOM has been by his side, supporting him as he passed the national exam and secured a spot at Arbaminch University, a prestigious public institution where only 8.4% of 674,000 students succeeded. We are incredibly proud of him and trust that God will guide him on this journey. ROTOM not only eases burdens but also plays a crucial role in breaking the cycle of poverty."

7 Years Supported
