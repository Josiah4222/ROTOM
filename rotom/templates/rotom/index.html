Our Story

Reach One Touch One Mission (ROTOM) Ethiopia was founded in 2017 by a dedicated individual inspired by the power of good deeds. ROTOM Ethiopia is a non-profit charitable organization officially registered as a civil society organization under certificate #3764. Our main office is located in Bishoftu, Ethiopia. Our core mission is to elevate the dignity and enhance the quality of life for vulnerable older adults and their dependents through holistic support and improvements in their living conditions.
