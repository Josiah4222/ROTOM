Our Story

Reach One Touch One Mission (ROTOM) Ethiopia is a dedicated non-profit organization established in 2017 by a group of compassionate individuals committed to making a positive impact in their community. Registered under NGO number #3764, our headquarters are located in Bishoftu, Ethiopia Kebele 05. Our core mission is to elevate the dignity and enhance the quality of life for vulnerable older adults and their dependents through holistic support and improvements in their living conditions.

Our Programs

Home Based Care

ROTOM Ethiopia provides a range of home-based support programs designed to enhance the quality of life for older persons in our community.

Center Based Care

Our center-based services deliver comprehensive assistance for elderly individuals who require more intensive support.

grandchildren support

We focus on empowering the younger generation through education and mentorship.
