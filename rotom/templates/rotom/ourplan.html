Our Plan

Strategic roadmap for expanding our impact and serving more vulnerable older persons and their dependents across Ethiopia.

Strategic Goals

Expand Home-Based Care

Reach more seniors in their homes with comprehensive care packages including health monitoring, nutrition support, and social engagement programs.

Strengthen Center-Based Services

Enhance our rehabilitation center with better facilities, specialized care programs, and increased capacity to serve more beneficiaries.

Education & Sponsorship Programs

Expand our champion program to support more students from vulnerable families through their educational journey.

Community Outreach & Awareness

Develop community-based awareness programs about elder care, and mobilize more volunteers and partners to join our mission.
