Our Achievements

Celebrating our milestones in serving seniors and communities.

Extending Our Reach

Since 2017, ROTOM Ethiopia has positively impacted the lives of 150+ vulnerable Ethiopians, expanding our reach and providing essential support to those in need. We serve 120+ elders through our home-based program and 30+ seniors in our center-based care, creating a comprehensive support network for our community's most vulnerable members.

Rescuing Elders from Homelessness

We have provided safety, warmth, and security to 30+ formerly homeless seniors in our center-based program, creating a nurturing environment where they can thrive. Our comprehensive care includes daily meals, medical attention, social activities, and most importantly, a loving community that restores their dignity and hope.

Empowering Future Generations

Through our integrated support approach, we have indirectly supported 200+ grandchildren and dependents of the seniors we serve. By providing educational support, school supplies, and mentorship programs, we are breaking the cycle of poverty and empowering the next generation to build bright futures for themselves and their families.
