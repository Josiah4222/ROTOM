Jemanesh's Journey

Joined 2019 Bishoftu

When Jemanesh first came to us, she was frail, malnourished, and had lost hope. Living alone with no family support, she struggled to meet her basic needs.

After joining our center-based care program, she received regular meals, medical attention, and most importantly - a loving community. Today, Jemanesh is thriving with a smile that lights up our center.

Tadesse's Transformation

Joined 2020 Bishoftu

Tadesse was found living on the streets, suffering from untreated health conditions and severe malnutrition. He had been abandoned by his family and had given up on life.

Our outreach team brought him to our center where he received immediate medical care. After months of dedicated support, Tadesse regained his strength and dignity. He now helps mentor other new arrivals.

Home-Based Care

Almaz's New Beginning

Joined 2021 Bishoftu

Almaz, a grandmother raising three orphaned grandchildren, was struggling to survive. She couldn't afford food, medicine, or school fees for the children.

Through our integrated support program, Almaz now receives regular home visits, medical care, and food assistance. Her grandchildren are enrolled in school with full support.
